# Branch point history

This record keeps the history of how the branch point's review rules came to be, with the owner's decisions behind them. It sits outside the reviewed text, so a stranger who reviews the branch point does not read it.

Status: record. Moved out of branch point v1.10 on 2026-11-02 by [evaluation case 015](../../eval/cases/015-ruleset-simplification-review-2026-11-02.md) change 8 (the history leaves the reviewed text). The two sections below hold v1.10's text as the owner accepted it on 2026-11-01; only their links changed, for this file's path. The [branch point](../branch-point.md) links this file from its Meta-review signals section.

Reader: RD-4 (a fresh agent session) or the human owner tracing why a rule of the branch point exists.

## Meta-review history, as v1.10 held it

The trigger of three rounds predated the five counts recorded by 2026-10-27. The five counts: the rounds on methodology PR #51, the subject of [case 005](../../eval/cases/005-meta-review-stranger-rounds-2026-10-26.md); the methodology's IR specification counts 1 and 2 ([review record](https://github.com/majodali/methodology/blob/main/docs/intermediate-representation.review.md)); branch point v1.4 ([review record](branch-point-v1.4.review.md)); branch point v1.5 ([review record](branch-point-v1.5.review.md)). The three-round trigger fired on two counts that converged without any step failing: branch point v1.5 and the IR specification's count 2. The owner dispatched no meta-review for those two counts. Every meta-review so far was foreshadowed by a signal in its review record: growth with an abandoned IR, a drift the check passed, rejected fixes. When the owner dispatches a meta-review, the meta-review session reads the review record and the text. The session reports, as an [evaluation case](../vocabulary.md#def-evaluation-case), whether the process needs a change. [Case 007](../../eval/cases/007-meta-review-full-ir-rounds-2026-10-27.md) reviewed methodology PR #52's rounds. The owner adopted case 008's five changes on 2026-10-27. The owner adopted case 009's nineteen changes on 2026-10-29. Three of the nineteen confirm three rules as written: the readable verdict without a finding-rate threshold, the 200-word restatement limit, and the limit of ten. [Case 010](../../eval/cases/010-meta-review-ir-spec-count-4-2026-10-30.md) reviewed count 4 of the methodology's intermediate-representation specification. [Case 011](../../eval/cases/011-meta-review-vision-count-2-2026-10-30.md) reviewed count 2 of the methodology's vision document. The owner adopted case 010's thirteen recommended changes and case 011's nine on 2026-10-30. Seven of the twenty-two amend the methodology's intermediate-representation specification as its count 5. A [process change](../vocabulary.md#def-process-change) is an adopted change to how the rounds or the meta-review run. Four of the seven are also among the nineteen process changes of v1.9. Case 010's change 15 confirms four rules as written. The four rules: the withdrawal at confirmation, the fourth signal, the final round's fixes at the limit, and the limit of ten. Case 011's change 4 confirms that the second signal counts [own mismatches](../vocabulary.md#def-own-mismatch). Cases 010 and 011 each declined one change of its own, an SP-01 calibration, and the declines stand. Case 008
proposed no change to the D1 row's rule on joining a rule to a reason by connective prose. [Case 012](../../eval/cases/012-meta-review-branch-point-v1.9-and-ir-spec-count-5-2026-10-30.md) reviewed branch point v1.9 and the methodology specification's count 5 in one task. The owner adopted case 012's fifteen changes on 2026-10-30. Thirteen of the fifteen are the process changes of v1.10: changes 1, 2 and 4 to 14. Change 3 keeps the 200-word restatement limit as written. Change 15 amends the [evaluation corpus](../../eval/README.md)'s Meta-review records section. Eleven of the fifteen amend the methodology specification as its count 6: changes 1, 2, 4 to 9, 11, 13 and 14. The meta-reviews so far are cases 005 and 007 to 012.

## The owner's decisions on case 005's held changes and their later fate, 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-30

Case 005's change 1, a verdict by a finding-rate threshold, is struck, because
the verdict rule reached zero contradictions without a threshold.
Case 005's change 3, a stop at a flat finding count, is struck because contradictions ran to round 9 while the rate was flat. Case 005's change 4 was replaced by the third signal on 2026-10-27, because words per IR claim measure prose drift from the IR. Change 11 of case 012 struck the third signal. Change 11 keeps the per-round word and claim counts as measures in the review record. The first, second and fourth signals keep their numbers.
Case 005's change 2, SP-18 calibrated, is adopted in the narrow
form: a duty names its actor and its trigger. The split of the methodology's IR specification into form and process is closed as not needed, because that specification reached readable at 2,808 words. Reviewing only the amended passages after round 1
stays not recommended. Fixing only new-passage findings stays not
recommended.
