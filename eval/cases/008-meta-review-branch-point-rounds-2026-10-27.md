My judgement.

Confirmation. Three rejections were procedure defects: round 5's edits aborted on a wrapped anchor and never reached the prose. Three were judgement defects: round 1's C17 and C26 fixes were incomplete, round 2's "the brief" kept two referents. Procedure defects need a check the author runs alone; the claims check is that check and it failed. Judgement defects need a second reader; the confirmation is that reader and it worked.

Claims check. It passed because its test is overlap between a changed claim and some sentence, and the unfixed row still overlapped C49's unchanged words. The test should also ask whether the matched sentence changed since the last round. A changed claim matched to an unchanged sentence is a miss.

Self-application. Fair and productive. The actor-less duties, "no other file" against the findings table, and the brief's SP-05 against the style specification's were defects of the procedure, found by running it. The limit: a stranger under the brief cannot find a defect in the brief's rules. The meta-review reads for that.

Six rounds. Rounds 1 to 3 descended. Round 4 had the fewest findings and three contradictions; a flat-stop rule would have stopped wrong, which confirms striking case 005's change 3. Rounds 5 and 6 went to fix-induced defects and the status line. About 12 of the 52 findings after round 1 sit on passages an earlier fix touched. The cost is acceptable for a normative text.

## Outcome

Proposed process changes.

1. The claims check reports a changed claim whose best-matching sentence did not change since the last round. Evidence: round 5, three fixes passed the check and failed the confirmation. Recommended.
2. Rounds step 6 drops "in one form": a restatement mismatch on the same passage in two rounds is a prose finding. Evidence: the status line in rounds 2, 4 and 6 never triggered the step. Recommended.
3. A finding declined in part is carried in the brief with the declined part and its reason. Evidence: round 2's finding 4 recurred in rounds 4 and 6; the one finding carried did not recur. Recommended.
4. During rounds the prose status line and the IR Status carry one state, the version under review and the round number, and the brief lists the form. Evidence: three rounds on the status line; round 6 finding 1 unfixed. Recommended.
5. The readable round's findings are fixed and confirmed by that round's instance before delivery; the record marks each confirmed or unfixed. Evidence: ten findings unfixed; the confirmation caught 10 defects in five runs. Recommended.
6. Change 6, connective prose, and the limit of ten stay as written. Evidence: neither fired in this count. No change.

Owner's decision on the outcome, 2026-10-27: changes 1 to 5 adopted; 6 as proposed, no change. Carried into branch point v1.5 and, for change 5, into the methodology's intermediate-representation specification.
